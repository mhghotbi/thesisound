# Source candidates

Verification date: **2026-08-10**. Each preferred and alternate package was checked against a publisher, institutional repository, library catalogue, DOI record, or a scan/transcript linked to one of those records. “Full text verified” means that the text was opened and its relevant material inspected; it does not mean redistribution is permitted. Page counts can vary between scans and HTML renderings.

## Redistribution labels

- **Commit-safe:** public domain or an open license was verified; preserve attribution, notices, and any source-specific conditions.
- **Conditional commit:** open access is verified, but repository use must be checked against non-commercial, share-alike, no-derivatives, third-party-content, or similar conditions.
- **Private fixture:** full text may be acquired for evaluation but should not be published in this repository.
- **Manifest/excerpt:** store a retrieval manifest and, only after review, a short locatable excerpt; do not commit the full work.
- **Unresolved:** do not freeze until the stated access, edition, provenance, or licensing problem is solved.
